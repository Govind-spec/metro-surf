METRO DASH mall-city environment update

Reference: WhatsApp Image 2026-10-25 at 16.07.09.jpeg

The environment now follows the reference's cyan/pink/yellow/cream palette:
- Recessed shop windows, framed JET SHOP and SUNNY YARD signs, bunny posters.
- Multi-level malls, glass balconies, escalators and elevated footbridges.
- Curved mall arches, cyan rail portals, hanging bunny/smiley banners.
- Planters, flowers, trailing balcony greenery, trees and curved palm fronds.
- Benches, lamps, shelters, bins and a rooftop bunny landmark.
- Warm sleepers, silver rails, track fasteners and yellow safety curbs.
- A blue gradient sky, white clouds, depth haze and glass city towers.
- Waterfront promenade, water highlights and a distant Ferris wheel.

The four existing visual zones and their scrolling cadence are retained.
Their themes are now Sunny Yard, Sky Mall, Rooftop Gardens and Bayview
Promenade. Background cross-trains have supporting decks that follow their
existing longitudinal motion. Playable train designs are unchanged.

Code:
  assets/environment/mall-city.js contains the new scenery factory.
  endless_runner.html loads it and delegates its environment builders to it.
  Only environment construction, visual zone labels and one background-deck
  transform update were changed in the existing game code.

Runner and train artwork, camera behavior, lighting setup, RIFT, input,
obstacle generation, collision detection, scoring and power-ups are
byte-for-byte unchanged from the pre-environment-update HTML. Scenery uses
its own palette-preserving materials and sky shader under the existing
sun/hemisphere lights. Near-world surfaces receive the existing shadows.

All new geometry, shop artwork, glass textures and signs are generated
locally. No external images, GLB models or new packages are required.
Repeated static details are batched by material; scenery templates share
geometry across the existing chunk pool. No new scene objects are allocated
by the scenery scrolling update.

Run locally:
  python3 -m http.server 8025 --bind 127.0.0.1
  http://127.0.0.1:8025/endless_runner.html

Inspect the actual production environment in each zone:
  http://127.0.0.1:8025/tests/environment-preview.html
Choose Gameplay, Balcony, Street or Overview. The preview stages the same
runner and trains for comparison and is not loaded by the playable game.

Regression checks (Node.js; no additional dependencies):
  node tests/environment-regression.cjs
  node tests/train-regression.cjs
The environment test also runs the existing runner, input, scoring,
power-up and collision checks. It validates 360 lane-clearance raycasts,
finite geometry, all four zones and a simulated ten-minute scroll at 68 m/s
with no chunk gaps, geometry growth or detached elevated rail decks.
An optional pre-update HTML path checks unchanged production source.

Browser checks (Playwright and the localhost server required):
  node tests/browser-smoke.cjs
Set CHROME_PATH when Chrome is not at the default macOS location.
The isolated headless browser checks WebGL compilation, keyboard and RIFT
dispatch, collisions, ramp ascent and roof running, and captures every
environment zone. Results and screenshots: tests/artifacts/environment.

Limits:
This is a procedural recreation of the reference's design and atmosphere,
not a pixel-identical reconstruction of the illustrated scene. Repetition,
layout and proportions are adapted to the existing three-lane game.
No original scene models, materials or lighting rig were supplied.
Existing font/CSS CDN dependencies still need packaging before the entire
game is offline-ready; the new scenery itself has no network dependencies.
Physical camera tracking and arcade-hardware frame rates were not tested.
